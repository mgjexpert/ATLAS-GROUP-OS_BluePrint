# 07 — Monetization & Business Model

## Thesis

Atlas should first become its own customer.

The preferred commercialization path:

```text
INTERNAL USE
 -> MANAGED SERVICE
 -> PRODUCTIZED SERVICE
 -> SAAS
```

## Business models

### Atlas Managed
Atlas operates a digital department for a customer.

Examples:
- Atlas CX — managed support/customer service.
- Atlas Sales — sales operations.
- Atlas Growth — marketing operations.
- Atlas Automation — process automation.
- Atlas Digital Factory — websites/apps/software.
- Atlas Content — editorial/media production.
- Atlas Research — research/intelligence.
- Atlas PMO — project operations.
- Atlas Backoffice — administrative operations.

The client buys an outcome/capacity/department, not a chatbot.

### Atlas OS SaaS
Customers operate their own organizations, teams, agents, tools, knowledge, workflows and approval policies through the platform.

### Atlas Ventures
Atlas creates and operates owned businesses/products.

Lifecycle:
idea -> research -> business case -> approval -> MVP -> market test -> kill/iterate/scale -> business unit.

## Branch as a Service

A signed client/service can trigger a controlled onboarding pipeline:

```text
contract approved
 -> create tenant/branch
 -> provision CRM
 -> provision knowledge
 -> provision agent packs
 -> configure channels
 -> configure workflows
 -> QA
 -> human approval
 -> go live
```

A branch may be:
- logical tenant on shared Atlas infrastructure;
- dedicated physical stack/VPS for larger or isolated clients.

## Internal suppliers

Shared Atlas units can supply owned ventures.

Example:
LeveLab requests an ebook -> Atlas Content provides research, writing, review and design.

This allows:
- reuse of specialist teams;
- cost allocation;
- service-level measurement;
- creation of external service products from capabilities proven internally.

## Unit economics

For each client/branch track:

```text
Revenue
- Model/API cost
- Infrastructure
- SaaS/external tools
- Human labor
- Agent/tool execution cost
= Contribution margin
```

Without cost attribution Atlas is a collection of agents.
With cost attribution, contracts and service delivery it becomes an operating company.
